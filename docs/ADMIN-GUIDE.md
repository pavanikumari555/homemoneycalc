# Admin panel guide (aasaan Hindi)

Ye guide owner ke liye hai. Koi technical knowledge nahi chahiye.

## Panel kaise kholein

1. Browser mein jaayein: https://homemoneycalc.com/admin/
2. Address khud badal ke `homemoneycalc-admin.pages.dev` ho jaayega. Ye sahi hai.
3. Apna username aur password daalein. Ye kabhi kisi ko chat ya message mein na bhejein.

## Panel ka layout

- **Left side (menu):** 8 sections ke buttons. Phone pe ye upar 2-2 ki line mein dikhte hain.
- **Right side:** jo section aapne chuna, uski jaankari.
- **Number wala gola** (jaise `2`): aapke liye utne kaam pending hain.
- **Peela dot:** us section mein dekhne layak baat hai, jaise Google impressions girna.
- Left menu ke neeche report ka time, **"Abhi refresh karein"** button aur **"Website dekhein"** link hai.

## Har section ka kaam

| Section | Kya milega | Kitni baar dekhein |
|---|---|---|
| Aaj ka haal | Website theek hai ya nahi, aaj ke visits, Google ke 7 din, aapke pending kaam | Roz 1 minute |
| Visits | Aaj/kal ke visitors, roz ki table, kaunse pages khule, log kahan se aaye | Hafte mein 1–2 baar |
| Google Search | Clicks, impressions, average position, roz ki table, log kya search karke aaye | Hafte mein 1 baar |
| Zaroori kaam | Aapke kaam (steps ke saath), developer ke fixes, approvals | Jab menu mein number dikhe |
| Share & Promotion | Pinterest pin aur email ke ready drafts, safe backlink research | Roz ya jab time ho |
| AdSense | Earning ki taiyaari ke steps. Apply abhi hold par hai | Mahine mein 1 baar |
| System & Safety | Automatic checks, technical details, aane wale calculators | Zaroorat par |
| Madad (Guide) | Ye guide chhoti shakal mein | Jab bhi confusion ho |

"Aaj ka haal" ke 4 boxes pe click karein to seedha us section mein pahunch jaayenge.

## Roz ka 5 minute ka routine

1. **Aaj ka haal** kholein. Upar ka box hara/normal ho to sab theek hai. Peela ho to padhein.
2. Menu mein **Zaroori kaam** pe number dikhe to kholein. Har kaam mein **"Steps dekhein"** dabayein aur 1-2-3 karke karein.
3. **Share & Promotion** kholein. Ek draft chunein aur neeche ke steps se post karein.

## Share draft kaise post karein

1. Draft ke saath **Copy** button dabayein.
2. Text ko apne shabdon mein thoda badlein. Har post ek jaisi na ho.
3. Draft ka link khol ke check karein ki page aur number sahi dikh rahe hain.
4. Apne Pinterest, Facebook Page ya Buffer mein paste karke post ya schedule karein.
5. Reddit ya Facebook group mein post karne se pehle us group ke rules padhein, aur bata dein ki ye aapki website hai.

Panel se kuch bhi apne aap post ya email nahi hota. Ye jaan-boojh kar hai, taaki account spam na maana jaaye.

## Email draft (bloggers/teachers) kaise bhejein

1. `[Name]` aur `[their post title]` jaise brackets asli jaankari se bharein.
2. Sirf ek insaan ko ek email bhejein. Bulk email nahi.
3. Jinhe bheja, unka record rakhwayein taaki dobara na bheja jaaye.

## Google Search ke numbers kaise samjhein

- **Impressions:** Google results mein aapki site kitni baar dikhi.
- **Clicks:** kitne logon ne click karke site kholi.
- **Position:** 1–10 matlab pehla page. Number jitna kam, utna behtar.
- Google ka data 2–3 din der se aata hai. Aakhri 2–3 din zero ya kam dikhein to ghabrayein nahi.
- Agar 2 din se zyada zero rahe, to panel khud **Zaroori kaam** mein check karne ke steps dega.

## Visits ke numbers kaise samjhein

- `—` ka matlab data nahi mila. Iska matlab zero visitors nahi hai.
- `0` ka matlab us reading mein koi visit record nahi hua.
- Kuch log browser mein tracking band rakhte hain, isliye asli visitors thode zyada ho sakte hain.

## Kya kabhi nahi karna

1. Password, API key ya token chat, WhatsApp ya email mein paste na karein.
2. Backlinks, followers ya traffic na kharidein. Google penalty laga sakta hai.
3. Apne hi ads pe click na karein (jab AdSense shuru ho).
4. Roz-roz website mein bade badlav na karwayein. Ek badlav karein, phir 2–4 hafte result dekhein.
5. Ek hi text har jagah copy-paste na karein.

## Kuch samajh na aaye to

1. **Abhi refresh karein** dabayein.
2. Upar peela "Report purani ya missing hai" dikhe, to **Zaroori kaam** mein report dobara chalane ke steps hain.
3. Phir bhi samajh na aaye, to us screen ka screenshot lekar developer/AI ko bhejein. Screenshot mein password na ho.
